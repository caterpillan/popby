How PopRound works

A short guide for new members. Takes about three minutes to read.

The idea: you tell the people you know where you will be and when. If they are free, they pop round. It is also a simple shared calendar for your family: a dentist appointment, a golf trip or the school play, so everyone knows who is where. No group-chat scrolling, no invitations to chase, and only people in your circles ever see anything.

1. Getting started

  1. Create your account with your email and a password. Choose a username. This is the name other people in your circles see. Your real name stays private.
  2. Add your family. Add the first names of the people who usually come along with you (children, partner, friends). Only first names, and you can change them any time.
  3. Join or create a circle (next section). You will not see anything until you are in one.
  4. Add PopRound to your Home Screen. On an iPhone, open PopRound in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. It then opens like any other app, and this is also what lets it send you alerts.

2. Circles

A circle is a private group of people who know each other: a class, a team, a street, a group of mates. Only people in a circle see its activities.

Join a circle

Tap the PopRound logo at the top left, then Find a circle. Type the 6-letter code a friend gave you and tap Find circle. If the organiser added a secret word check, pick the right word from the mixed-up list (you get one try). Then tap Request to join. The organiser has to approve you, so it can take a little while.

Create a circle

Tap the logo, then Create a new circle. Give it a name, and choose a look. You can add a short description, the town or postcode start where the circle is based (optional, never a street address), an end date (handy for a school year or a season) and a secret word check. When it is made, tap Share this code and send the code to the people you want in.

Approving people

Requests to join appear under Waiting for your approval. Only approve people you know in real life. Do not recognise someone? Tap Decline, or Block if you are worried.

Making it yours

  • Your own name for a circle: only you see it.
  • Keep at the top: open a circle and use this to keep it first in your list.
  • Tap a circle name at the top of the home screen to see only that circle's activities.
  • Leave this circle any time from inside the circle.

3. Share an activity

Tap Share an activity on the home screen.

  • Type: Pop round means friends turn up if they are free and nobody has to reply. RSVP means friends reply Going or Maybe, so you know numbers. Use RSVP for things like a birthday party.
  • What and where: write a short title, like Playground. For the place you can type an address or meeting spot, or tap Choose on a map to pick a spot in your maps app. People who tap the place open it in their own maps. You can type a place instead if it is not on a map.
  • When: choose the day (Calendar, Today or Tomorrow) and the time it starts. An end time is optional. With no end time, it fades out a few hours after it starts.
  • Who is coming from your family: tick the people you are bringing, so friends know the numbers.
  • Share with: tick one or more circles. Only people in those circles see it. Someone in several of them sees it once.
  • Hide from specific people (optional): choose people who will not see it. They are not told.
  • Let people copy it (Pop round only): other people in your circles can copy your activity into their own circles. You can switch this off for any activity.

Tap Send and everyone in those circles gets an alert.

Use it as a family planner

PopRound is not only for meet-ups. It also works as a simple shared calendar for your family, so people you live with, or look after, know where you will be.

  1. Make a circle just for your family (for example “Our family”) and share its code with your partner, grandparents or anyone who should be in the loop. You approve each person.
  2. When you have a dentist appointment, a golf trip or the school play, tap Share an activity and choose the family circle. Pick Pop round if people only need to know, or RSVP if you want to know who is coming.
  3. Choose the day and time and, if you like, where it is. Your family sees it straight away and gets an alert.

Anything you share only goes to the circles you tick, so you can tell your family about a dentist visit without telling your friends. PopRound does not add things to your phone's own calendar app yet.

Links: PopRound only allows a map link in the place field. Links are blocked in the other text boxes, to keep everyone safe.

4. Replying to an activity

Open an activity on the home screen and choose:

  • See you there (Pop round) or Going (RSVP).
  • I might pop by (Pop round) or Maybe (RSVP).
  • Not for me, which hides it. The host is not told. You can bring it back from Hidden.

When you say Going or Maybe, you choose who from your family is coming. Other people only see your username with a number, like Sean +2, so nobody else sees your children's names. The organiser can see who is coming. You can change your reply any time.

5. Changing plans

  • Edit or delete: if you organise an activity, open it and tap Edit or Delete. When you save an edit, tap Save and notify so people in the circle know it has changed.
  • Cancel: choose Cancel for everyone, or Let others keep it going. That passes the activity to someone else for a short time so it can still happen. People who replied are told it is cancelled.
  • If someone cancels an activity you replied to: you will see a card. Tap Keep it going to take it over and become the host, or No thanks, hide this to remove it from your screen.
  • Duplicate: on a Pop round that allows copies, tap Duplicate to put it in your own circles. You become the organiser of your copy, and the original organiser is not told. You will not see both on your screen.

6. Alerts and reminders

  • Go to Settings, then Notifications, and turn them on. You will get an alert when someone posts, edits or cancels an activity in your circles.
  • Alert me at: on an activity you are going to, you can set a reminder for when it is time to leave.
  • Notifications need PopRound to be added to your Home Screen (see getting started).

7. Privacy and safety

  • Who can see your name: by default only the organisers of circles you are in can see your name and the family names you bring. Everyone else sees your username and a number. Change it in Settings, under Privacy & safety.
  • Hide my activities from: choose people who never see what you share.
  • Report or block: for a real problem, tap Report or block on one of their activities, or next to their name in a circle. After a block you cannot see each other's activities, replies or names, and they are not told.
  • Removing someone: organisers can remove a person from their circle. They get a short, neutral notice.
  • PopRound is for adults. Please read the Community rules and the Privacy policy.

8. Your account

  • Change your username or family names: Settings, then Profile.
  • Change your password: Settings, Profile, then Account. The eye button lets you check what you typed.
  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in page tap Forgot your password?. We email you a link to choose a new one.
  • Download my data: Settings, Profile, Account. You get a copy of what PopRound holds about you.
  • Delete my account: also under Account. It removes your account, your activities and your replies for good, so download your data first if you want a copy.
  • Appearance: choose light or dark, and your colours, in Settings.

9. Help

Stuck, or something not working? Open Settings and tap Help & feedback to email us. Tell us what you were doing and what happened. If PopRound looks out of date, close it fully and reopen it.
