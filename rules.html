Community rules

Draft for the private testing phase. Last updated 18 October 2026.

In short: PopRound is for friendly meet-ups between people who know each other.

Not allowed

  • Offensive, abusive, sexual or threatening language, including in names, titles and notes.
  • Harassing or targeting anyone, or sharing someone else’s details without their agreement.
  • Pretending to be someone else.
  • Adverts or spam that are not an approved sponsor.
  • Anything illegal, or that puts a child at risk.

How we help

  • The app asks you to reword text that contains offensive words.
  • Tap Report or block under an activity, or Report / block next to someone in a circle. Blocking hides each of you from the other, and they are not told.

What happens after a report

We read every report. We may warn someone, remove content, remove them from a circle, or close their account. We may not be able to tell you the outcome, to protect the privacy of the people involved.

In an emergency

PopRound is not an emergency service. If someone is in danger, call 999 (UK) or your local emergency number.
