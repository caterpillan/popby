Privacy policy

Draft for the private testing phase. Last updated 18 October 2026.

In short: PopRound is a private app for adults who know each other, mostly parents. We collect as little as we can, we do not sell your data, and we do not show your name to people outside your circles unless you choose.

Who we are

PopRound is run by Sean James Pillan (“we”). Contact: see “Contact us” in the app’s Settings.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address, a password (stored securely, we cannot read it), and the username you choose.
  • Family first names: the first names of children or family members you add, so you can say who is going. We ask only for first names.
  • Your activities and replies: the title, place, time and note of activities you share, and your Going or Maybe replies.
  • Circles: the circles you create or join, and who approved whom.
  • Optional first and last name: if you add them in your Profile, only you can see them.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a code that lets your phone receive alerts.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report someone or block them, we keep a record so we can act on it.

Who can see your name

By default, your username and the first names of the family you bring to an activity are visible only to people in circles you organise, and to the organisers of circles you belong to. Everyone else sees “A member”. You can choose to show your name to everyone in Settings.

Sponsor cards and local ads

PopRound shows sponsor cards. To show local ones, we use your phone’s time zone and language to guess your country. If you type a town, area or postcode in Settings, it is stored only on your phone and never sent to us. If you leave it empty, we may match place names on activities you can see to local sponsors. Sponsors never receive your name or contact details. We count how many times a sponsor card is shown, tapped or hidden, by country and area, with no link to you.

App usage

We record which days you open the app, so we can improve it and spot security problems. We do not record what you do inside the app for this purpose. These records are deleted after 90 days.

Who else handles your data

We use Supabase to store data and run the service, and GitHub Pages to host the app. They process data on our behalf. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Your data stays while your account exists. Circles and their activities are removed after the end date the organiser sets. You can delete your account yourself in the app (Settings, then Profile, then Account). That deletes your account, family members, replies and settings straight away, and the activities you organise. Circles you organise are handed to another member, or deleted if nobody else is in them. Reports you made about others are kept for safety, without your name.

Your rights

You can download a copy of your data in the app (Settings, then Profile, then Account). You can also ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Contact us using the details in the app. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) if you are in the UK.

Children

PopRound is for adults. Children do not have accounts. Parents may add a child’s first name only.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we will say so in the app.
